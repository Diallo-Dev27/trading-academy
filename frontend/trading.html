Trading Basics

01

What is a Market?

A financial market is a place physical or virtual where buyers and sellers exchange assets such as stocks, currencies, or commodities. Prices are determined by supply and demand in real time.

02

How Orders Work

A market order executes immediately at the current price. A limit order executes only at your specified price or better. A stop order triggers when the price hits a certain level — essential for protecting positions.

03

Reading a Chart

Charts show price movement over time. Candlestick charts are the most popular: each candle shows the open, close, high, and low for a given period. Green candles = price rose; red = price fell.

04

Leverage & Margin

Leverage lets you control a larger position with a smaller deposit. For example, 10:1 leverage means $100 controls $1,000 in assets. While it amplifies gains, it equally amplifies losses — use it with great care.

05

Bid / Ask & Spread

The bid is the price buyers offer; the ask is what sellers want. The difference is the spread — your immediate cost when entering a trade. Lower spread = cheaper trading.

06

Timeframes

Traders analyze different timeframes from 1-minute charts for scalping to weekly charts for long-term positions. Using multiple timeframes (top-down analysis) gives a fuller picture of market context.

// Module 02

What is Trading?

Definition

Trading is the act of buying and selling financial assets stocks, forex, crypto, commodities with the goal of generating profit from price movements. Unlike long-term investing, trading is more active and reactive to market conditions.

Traders use a combination of technical analysis (reading charts and patterns), fundamental analysis (news, earnings, economic data), and market sentiment to make decisions.

Types of Trading

⚡
Scalping
Seconds to minutes. Very high frequency.
☀️
Day Trading
Open & close same day. No overnight risk.
📈
Swing Trading
Days to weeks. Rides market swings.
🏦
Position / Investing
Months to years. Fundamental driven.

Key Markets to Trade

The most popular markets for retail traders are: Forex (the largest with $7 trillion daily volume), Crypto (24/7, highly volatile), Stocks & Indices (equities, ETFs), and Commodities (gold, oil, silver). Each market has its own characteristics — liquidity, volatility, trading hours, and fees. Choose based on your schedule and personality.

// Module 03

Risk Management

Professional traders say: "Protect your capital first, profits will follow." No strategy works without proper risk management. Here are the essential rules:

🎯

The 1–2% Rule

Never risk more than 1–2% of your total capital on a single trade. This ensures that even a streak of 10 losing trades won't destroy your account. Consistency beats gambling.

🛡️

Always Use a Stop Loss

A stop loss is a predefined exit point that automatically closes your trade when price moves against you. It removes emotion from the equation and caps your downside.

⚖️

Risk/Reward Ratio

Only take trades with at least a 1:2 risk/reward ratio — meaning you risk $50 to potentially gain $100. With a 50% win rate, a 1:2 RR is already profitable.

🚫

Avoid Overtrading

More trades ≠ more profit. Overtrading leads to higher fees, more emotional decisions, and faster losses. Trade only high-probability setups that match your strategy.

📓

Keep a Trading Journal

Record every trade: entry, exit, reason, emotions, result. Reviewing your journal regularly reveals patterns — both in the market and in your own behavior.

Impact of Risk per Trade

10% risk per tradeSurvives ~10 losses

* Even the best traders have losing streaks. Lower risk per trade = longer survival = more time to learn and recover.

// Module 04

Trading Psychology

😱

Fear & FOMO

Fear of missing out drives impulsive entries without proper analysis. Fear of loss causes traders to exit winners too early or hold losers too long. Recognize these emotions — they are your biggest enemies.

🤑

Greed & Overconfidence

After a series of wins, many traders increase size recklessly or abandon their rules. Overconfidence is a silent account killer. Stay humble — the market humbles everyone eventually.

🔁

Revenge Trading

Losing a trade and immediately jumping back in to "recover" the loss is one of the most destructive behaviors. It's emotionally-driven, not strategy-driven. After a loss, step away and reset your mind.

🧘

Discipline & Patience

The best traders spend more time waiting than trading. A strategy that wins 40% of the time can still be highly profitable with the right risk/reward. Stick to your plan — every single trade.

"The goal of a successful trader is to make the best trades. Money is secondary."
— Alexander Elder, Trading for a Living
// Quick Reminders

Golden Rules of Trading

⏳

Be Patient

Wait for your setup to come to you. If it's not there, there is no trade. Chasing the market costs money.

📋

Follow Your Plan

Write your trading plan before the session starts. Define your entries, exits, and risk in advance — not while in the trade.

🧊

Stay Emotionless

Your job is to execute your strategy, not to be right or wrong. Detach your ego from your trades and treat each one as a business decision.

📚

Never Stop Learning

Markets evolve constantly. The best traders are lifelong students — always reading, reviewing, and refining their edge.
